“The best opportunities should reach every artist — not just those who already know where to look.” — Artatlas

Artatlas curates the world's most significant art exhibitions alongside the best opportunities for artists — residencies, grants, open calls and fellowships — in one place, updated weekly.

The platform exists because the art world runs on information, and information is rarely distributed equally. Artatlas is an attempt to change that.
