About Artatlas

Built for artists.
Loved by art.

Artatlas was built from a simple frustration: the best artistic opportunities existed but were scattered, hidden behind newsletters and paywalls, accessible only to those who already knew where to look.

We built the platform we wished existed. A single place where artists find residencies, grants and open calls, and where art lovers discover the exhibitions worth travelling for.

Updated every week. Free forever.
