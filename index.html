"The best opportunities should reach every artist - not just those who already know where to look." - Artatlas

Artatlas curates the world's most significant art exhibitions alongside the best opportunities for artists - residencies, grants, open calls and fellowships - in one place, updated weekly.

The platform exists because the art world runs on information, and information is rarely distributed equally. A grant that could change a career goes unnoticed. An exhibition worth travelling for closes before anyone hears about it. Artatlas is an attempt to change that.

New exhibitions and opportunities are added every two weeks. The newsletter goes out every Monday.

To suggest an exhibition or opportunity, or to get in touch: hello@artatlas.world
